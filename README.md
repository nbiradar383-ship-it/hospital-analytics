# 🏥 Hospital Patient Analytics & Treatment Outcome Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital patient data to uncover insights related to patient admissions, treatment outcomes, costs, and overall hospital performance.
Interactive dashboards are built using **Tableau** to support data-driven decision-making for healthcare management.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze patient admission and discharge trends
* Measure recovery rate and mortality rate
* Evaluate treatment costs across diseases and departments
* Assess doctor and department performance
* Build interactive dashboards with filters and drill-down features

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset includes the following fields:

* Patient_ID
* Patient_Name
* Gender
* Age
* Disease
* Department
* Doctor
* Admission_Date
* Discharge_Date
* Treatment_Cost
* Recovery_Status (Recovered / Under Treatment / Expired)
* State & District

---

## 📊 Dashboards

### 1️⃣ Hospital Overview

* Total Patients (KPI)
* Total Treatment Cost
* Average Length of Stay
* Recovery Rate & Mortality Rate
* Monthly Admission Trends
* Admissions by Department
* Patients by City (Map)

---

### 2️⃣ Disease Analysis

* Patient Count by Disease
* Average Treatment Cost by Disease
* Recovery Rate by Disease
* Disease vs Department Heatmap

---

### 3️⃣ Doctor & Department Performance

* Patient Count by Doctor
* Recovery Rate by Doctor
* Average Length of Stay per Doctor
* Department-wise Treatment Cost

---

### 4️⃣ Patient Journey & Outcomes

* Admission vs Discharge Trends
* Recovery Status Distribution
* Treatment Cost by City & State
* Interactive Filters

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Certain diseases contribute to higher patient volume and treatment cost
* Recovery rates vary significantly across doctors and departments
* Some cities show higher hospital admissions and costs
* Departments differ in efficiency based on patient stay duration

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Allocate more resources to high-demand departments
* Improve treatment processes for low recovery rate cases
* Optimize costs for high-expense diseases
* Expand facilities in high-admission regions
